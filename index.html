Getting Here

✈ Nearest Airports
Belfast International (BFS)
The closest airport. Approx. 45 min drive to Old Rectory Killyman. Direct flights from many European cities.
~45 min
George Best Belfast City (BHD)
Belfast's city airport. About 1hr by car. Great connections from UK and Ireland.
~1h
Dublin Airport (DUB)
The region's largest hub with global flights. Approx. 2hr drive to venue. Best option for long-haul travellers.
~2h
City of Derry Airport (LDY)
Smaller northern airport. About 1hr 10min drive. Flights from Manchester, London and Glasgow.
~1h 10min
🚗 Getting to the Venue
By Car
The best way to get around the area. Free parking available at the venue. We recommend using GPS — the address is Old Rectory, Killyman Road, Dungannon, Co. Tyrone, BT71 6RF.
Transfer / Taxi
Local taxis available in Dungannon. We recommend booking in advance for the weekend. Details on any organised transfers will be shared closer to the date.
From Belfast
The Goldline coach connects Belfast to Dungannon with regular departures. From Dungannon, a local taxi takes around 10 min to the venue. We recommend pre-booking your taxi.

Attire

Dress Code

♀
Guests (Ladies)
Formal / Morning Dress optional
Formal dress or elegant midi welcome. Morning dress is optional. Please avoid white, ivory and champagne tones. Heels may be swapped after the ceremony — the grounds are garden-style.
♂
Guests (Gentlemen)
Lounge Suit / Morning Dress optional
Lounge suit with tie is the dress code. Morning dress is welcome for those who prefer it. Please avoid casual attire.

The wedding takes place in June in Northern Ireland — the weather can surprise! We recommend bringing an elegant layer for outdoor moments. Old Rectory's beautiful gardens will be part of the celebration.

Northern Ireland

Explore Northern Ireland

Make the most of your trip and explore one of the most beautiful corners of the British Isles.

🏰
SOPHIE'S VILLAGE
Hillsborough Castle

The official Royal residence in Northern Ireland, in the charming village of Hillsborough — Sophie's hometown. Tour the elegant State Rooms and 100 acres of stunning gardens, just 20 min from Belfast.

Learn more ↗
🌊
UNESCO WORLD HERITAGE
Giant's Causeway

Northern Ireland's iconic hexagonal basalt columns on the North Coast — one of the world's most breathtaking landscapes. Legend has it they were built by the giant Finn McCool. About 1hr 20min from the venue.

Learn more ↗
🌉
CARRICK-A-REDE
Rope Bridge

A rope bridge suspended 30 metres above the Atlantic Ocean, connecting the mainland to a tiny island. An unforgettable experience on the North Coast, close to the Giant's Causeway.

Learn more ↗
🏙️
CAPITAL CITY
Belfast

A vibrant city with rich history, street art, great food and the world-famous Titanic Museum. The Cathedral Quarter and the Merchant Quarter are unmissable. 1hr from the venue.

Learn more ↗
🛣️
SCENIC ROUTE
Causeway Coastal Route

One of the world's most beautiful coastal drives, linking Belfast to Derry City. Medieval castles, dramatic cliffs, picturesque villages and Game of Thrones filming locations along the way.

Learn more ↗
🏰
MEDIEVAL CASTLE
Dunluce Castle

A breathtaking medieval castle perched on clifftops above the sea. Said to have inspired Pyke in Game of Thrones. One of the most photogenic spots in all of Ireland.

Learn more ↗
